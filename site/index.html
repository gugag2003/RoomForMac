RoomForMac

A calm, native Mac cleaner. Smart Clean, an Uninstaller and live Status, in one app.

Download for Mac

macOS 26 Tahoe or later · Apple silicon and Intel


SHA-256

Before you open it

The first time you open RoomForMac, macOS says it can’t verify the app. RoomForMac is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to approve it once, in System Settings. It takes three steps and about a minute.

Read the Open Anyway guide

What it does

  • Smart Clean scans, shows you what it found and removes only what you confirm.
  • Uninstaller finds an app’s leftovers and moves the app to the Trash.
  • Status shows live processor, memory, disk, network and battery readings, in the app and in the menu bar.
