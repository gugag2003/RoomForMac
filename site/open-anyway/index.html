Open RoomForMac the first time

macOS blocks apps that Apple has not notarized. RoomForMac is one of them. Approve it once in System Settings, in three steps, and it opens with a double-click from then on.

  1. Try to open it

    Open RoomForMac from your Applications folder. If you are still in the disk image, drag RoomForMac to Applications first. macOS shows this message:

    “RoomForMac” Not Opened

    Apple could not verify “RoomForMac” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

    Click Done. Do not click Move to Trash.

  2. Open Privacy & Security

    Choose Apple menu, then System Settings. Click Privacy & Security in the sidebar and scroll down to Security. Next to this line, click Open Anyway:

    “RoomForMac” was blocked to protect your Mac.

    The button is there for about an hour after step 1. If it is gone, do step 1 again.

    Open Privacy & Security

    Or by hand: Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security → Security.

  3. Confirm

    macOS asks you once more:

    Open “RoomForMac”?

    Apple is not able to verify that it is free from malware that could harm your Mac or compromise your privacy. Don’t open this unless you are certain it is from a trustworthy source.

    Click Open Anyway. When macOS asks for your login password, or for Touch ID, give it and click OK. The password box is macOS’s own. It reads:

    Privacy & Security

    You are attempting to open an app that may cause harm to your Mac or compromise your privacy

    RoomForMac opens. From now on, open it like any other app.

The drawings only show where to click. Your Mac’s windows may look a little different. Apple’s own instructions: Open a Mac app from an unknown developer and Safely open apps on your Mac.

Is this safe?

Open Anyway is Apple’s own way to approve an app that is not notarized. It makes an exception for this one app and nothing else.

RoomForMac is open source, so anyone can read what it does: it is on GitHub. Every release lists the SHA-256 checksum of its download. Approve only a copy you downloaded from this site or from the project’s release page.

RoomForMac is signed with its own certificate, not an Apple one. macOS uses that signature to recognise the app after an update, so the permissions you gave it stay in place.

We never ask you to paste commands into Terminal

The three steps above use System Settings only. If a web page, a video or a message tells you to run a command to open RoomForMac, do not: it did not come from us.

If something goes wrong

There is no Open Anyway button

The button appears only after macOS has blocked the app, and it stays for about an hour. Do step 1 again, then go straight to step 2.

“RoomForMac” is damaged and can’t be opened.

The download is incomplete or was changed on the way. Delete it and download RoomForMac again.

It opens from the disk image, but it can’t update

RoomForMac has to live in your Applications folder to update itself. Drag it from the disk image to Applications, eject the disk image, and open the copy in Applications.
